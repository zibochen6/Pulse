# Pulse 0.1.21 (build 25)

- Use Settings → AI services to show or hide 88VIP and DeepSeek balances independently. Turning off a balance hides its Home card and pauses automatic refresh. Connection settings and saved credentials remain available.
- 在「设置 → AI 服务」可以分别开关 88VIP 和 DeepSeek 的余额显示。关闭后隐藏首页余额卡片并暂停自动刷新，仍可管理已有连接。
