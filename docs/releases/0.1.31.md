# Pulse 0.1.31 (build 35)

- Home opens on Obsidian Today Tasks or the selected Pulse list. Completed
  tasks start folded, and pending Obsidian changes stay visible. Short lists
  fit the panel and long lists scroll with Add within reach.
- AI services appear below the task list, with service problems surfaced in
  the summary. The overview leads to quota windows, reset times, exact
  balances in each reported currency, and service-specific recovery actions.

## 中文

- 首页先显示 Obsidian 今日待办或当前本地清单。已完成项默认收起，待同步项继续显示。短清单随内容收起，长清单滚动时「添加」始终可见。
- 待办下方可以打开 AI 服务概览，有异常时会在入口显示服务和原因。详情提供各额度窗口、重置时间、原币种余额，以及对应的重试或连接设置。
