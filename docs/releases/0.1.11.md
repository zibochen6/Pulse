# Pulse 0.1.11 (build 15)

## Gemini model quotas

Open **Settings → AI services → Track Gemini model quotas** to enable the
Gemini card on Home. The card shows the remaining five-hour allowance.
Select it to see reset times, the shared Gemini weekly allowance and the
separate Gemini 4 Argon weekly allowance when your account reports it.

Pulse reads the official `agy` usage report using your local Antigravity
sign-in. The card supports manual refresh and automatic checks every 10
minutes. Antigravity CLI 1.1.11 or later is required.

## Download or update

If Pulse is already installed, open **Settings → Updates → Check for updates**.
The [Pulse 0.1.11 release](https://github.com/zibochen6/Pulse/releases/tag/v0.1.11)
also includes a DMG for manual installation. This Apple Development-signed
preview supports macOS 13 and later on Apple Silicon and Intel.
