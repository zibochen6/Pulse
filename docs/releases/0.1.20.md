# Pulse 0.1.20 (build 24)

- The menu bar panel and Settings now share a paper-style layout, pastel provider cards, and clear light and dark palettes.
- Obsidian Today shows completion progress on a folded-corner checklist. Both Today and Pulse's local lists use square checkmarks and a centered add button that opens the inline composer.
- Usage, recording edits, and tag management share the new card and action styles.

- 菜单栏与设置采用个人看板的纸面风格，AI 用量用浅色卡片展示，跟随系统切换明暗外观。
- Obsidian 今日待办显示完成数。今日待办和本地待办都使用方形勾选框，点击卡底加号即可在列表内输入。
- 用量详情、口播剪辑和标签管理也换上了统一的卡片和按钮。
