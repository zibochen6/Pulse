# Pulse 0.1.14 (build 18)

- A paired Obsidian vault makes Today Tasks the only task list in Pulse. This also applies while Obsidian is closed and edits are waiting to sync.
- Disconnect the vault in Settings → Tasks to return to your existing native lists and tags.
- 接入 Obsidian 后，Pulse 只显示「今日待办」。在「设置 → 待办」断开知识库，即可回到原有本地清单。

Open **Settings → Updates → Check for updates** in Pulse, or download the DMG from the [Pulse 0.1.14 release](https://github.com/zibochen6/Pulse/releases/tag/v0.1.14). The Apple Development-signed preview supports macOS 13 and later on Apple Silicon and Intel.
