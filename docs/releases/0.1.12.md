# Pulse 0.1.12 (build 16)

- The focus timer starts with a quiet background when opened by mouse and shows a single focus cue during keyboard navigation. The countdown and cancel label fit the same button width.
- Home gives usage cards balanced columns and aligns the task list and its controls. Long balances keep every digit.
- Page changes use a brief fade without capturing the outgoing page. Short refreshes no longer flash a progress indicator, and lengthy refreshes keep the button in place.
- 开窗时专注按钮去掉了双层焦点框。额度卡对齐，待办布局更统一，切页和刷新减少了跳动。

## Download or update

Open **Settings → Updates → Check for updates** in Pulse, or download the DMG from the [Pulse 0.1.12 release](https://github.com/zibochen6/Pulse/releases/tag/v0.1.12). The Apple Development-signed preview supports macOS 13 and later on Apple Silicon and Intel.
