# Pulse 0.1.30 (build 34)

- Quick Copy sorts by successful copies in the last 30 days. The most recently
  copied entry comes first when counts match. Reopening Quick Copy refreshes the
  order, and entries stay in place while you use the list.

## 中文

- 快速复制按最近 30 天的成功复制次数排序。次数相同时，最近复制的排在前面。重新打开列表时刷新排序，点击复制时条目留在原位。
